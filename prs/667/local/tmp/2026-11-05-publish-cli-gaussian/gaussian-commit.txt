Test untruncated Gaussian tails statistically

Replace the invalid deterministic 5.5-sigma maximum assertion with a
binomial tail-count check using the analytic normal probability and a
Bernstein/union bound. Keep the RMS tolerances and finite z cutoff, and
check finiteness in all six coordinates.

All eight Gaussian cases pass five repeats at 1, 4 and 16 OpenMP threads,
and two-rank runs at 1 and 4 threads. A temporary 3-sigma clipping negative
control is rejected by the new assertions.
