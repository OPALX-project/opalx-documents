Batch independent FFT2D5 slice transforms within OPALX

Add --2D5BatchSize as an explicit positive-integer option, retaining serial
slice execution by default. Reuse native heFFTe R2C batch calls and contiguous
buffers without changing IPPL or transforming the longitudinal direction.
Preserve the existing Green function, normalization, potential halo and field
differences; keep diagnostic callbacks on the serial path.

Resize reused OPALX fields through updateLayout when the transverse grid
changes. Test partial batches, empty/neutral/vacated slices, anisotropic grids,
potential halos, unchanged sources and complete solves under both boundary modes.

Validation: four unit targets pass with one and four OpenMP threads, ten CLI
checks pass, and two-rank execution remains rejected. The identical 100-step
pilot drops from 21.38 s to about 11.02 s for batch sizes 8 and 32, with
RMS/emittance differences below 2.2e-14 relative. Host execution only; no
16-turn runs.
