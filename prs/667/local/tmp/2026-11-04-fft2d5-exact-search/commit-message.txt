Accelerate FFT2D5 nearest-segment search without changing projections

Build a per-solve hierarchy of segment bounding boxes and seed its search
with the previous segment. Use conservative floating-point pruning and
lowest-index tie handling, with an exhaustive fallback for stack exhaustion.

Compare segment indices and projected coordinates exactly with an independent
exhaustive scan, including intersections, ties, stale seeds and extreme scales.
Document the search, cache lifetime, numerical guard and memory cost.

Validation: four unit targets pass with one and four OpenMP threads; the
unsupported two-rank check passes. The unchanged 100-step DBA pilot drops
from 32.71 s to 21.27–21.81 s. Projection time falls from 10.86 s to 0.396 s;
tracking differences remain at floating-point reduction roundoff.
