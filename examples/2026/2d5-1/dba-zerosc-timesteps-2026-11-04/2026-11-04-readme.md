# DBA ZeroSC timestep study

The largest tested fixed step meeting the full-record ~1% tune-change criterion is **4 ps**. The reference is 2 ps. At 4 ps, the radial 95th-percentile tune change is 0.796% and the error against the analytic lattice is 0.762%. At 8 ps these rise to 1.383% and 1.367%.

**3 ps is the conservative choice when half-record stability must also be within 1%.** Its radial 95th-percentile first-half/second-half change is 0.892%; at 4 ps it is 2.083%. The median alone conceals noisy weak radial signals. The full-record result is not a guarantee of 1% accuracy for every particle or shorter record.

## Full-ring check

An independent source uses 96 particles over 48 equally spaced longitudinal positions, with an antipodal KV-shell pair at each position. After eight turns at 4 ps, all 96 particles have valid station-crossing records. Median tunes are Qx=0.769779 and Qy=1.689530. The 95th-percentile errors against analytic are 0.611% and 0.00653%. The maximum relative momentum-magnitude change is 3.2e-8. This checks source-position sensitivity; it is not a 100,000-particle space-charge run. The radial half-record diagnostic remains about 2.14%.

## Method and limits

- Executable: `/Users/adelmann/git/opalx/build-omp/src/opalx`, SHA-256 `4fd718db8dea11088409158506cd61761eb35f7a0e6a15638fba235c433ba0eb`. This is the same build as the current-build PR repeat. Its source patch hash is unchanged. No code changes or rebuilds were made for this study.
- All final scan points use one OpenMP thread and one MPI rank. Four-thread coarse attempts are retained in the raw study but excluded from the final timestep comparison. Coarse hard-edge trajectories are sensitive to rounding and thread count; fine-step multi-thread convergence is not established here.
- Space charge is disabled (`TYPE=NONE`); no FFT2D5 field solve is performed. Native checkpoint initialization selects ordinary fixed Boris/PIC integration, matching the path used with FFT2D5. Fresh ZeroSC ring tracking instead permits boundary subdivision, so its nominal timestep cannot be compared directly with these fixed steps.
- The section source has 64 matched KV particles, one central particle and four 10/50 micrometre probes. Acceptance uses the 64 KV particles. The probes are retained as diagnostics and excluded from KV selection. ZeroSC trajectories are independent; this is a timestep diagnostic, not a particle-number study.
- For each particle, laboratory trajectories are reconstructed and sampled at its own crossings of six equivalent cell entrances. A sinusoid frequency fit gives the full tunes; Twiss-normalized phase provides an independent check. Exact linear-lattice synthetic trajectories recover the analytic tunes to better than 2e-8, confirming the extraction and integer tune branch.
- The full-record criterion requires all 64 station records plus <=1% at the 95th percentile for analytic error, paired change from 2 ps and agreement of the two estimators. The separate half-record criterion is recorded explicitly. Two picoseconds is the finest tested reference, not a proven zero-step limit.
- Transverse deletion is disabled to avoid testing ring particles against unrelated element apertures; field apertures are retained. This is not a physical aperture-loss test. At coarse steps, missing station records correspond to large excursions beyond the 10 cm extraction window; particle IDs are still audited.
- One nanosecond was rejected before tracking by the existing requirement for ten steps through the shortest quadrupole (limit about 916 ps). No numerical tolerance or guard was relaxed.

At 4 ps there are approximately 294,930 fixed steps per turn; at 3 ps, 393,240. The 69-particle, 16-turn runs took 239 s and 308 s. The 96-particle full-ring check took 482 s for eight turns. These are diagnostic wall times with other study jobs running concurrently, not a performance estimate for 100,000 particles with space charge.
