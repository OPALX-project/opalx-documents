# DBA space-charge feasibility pilot

A 16-turn run is not practical on this laptop with the current fixed-step configuration. The measured four-thread cost projects to **about 54 days at 3 ps**, or **40 days at 4 ps**, per charge case. No full-turn campaign was launched.

## Measured cost

| OpenMP threads | Space charge | 100-step wall time | Approx. steady cost/step | Projected 16 turns at 3 ps |
|---:|:---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | On | 167.8 s | 1.660 s | 121 days |
| 4 | On | 75.4 s | 0.736 s | 54 days |
| 4 | Off | 5.0 s | 0.031 s | 2.3 days |

The ZeroSC baseline uses the same charged-matched particle source and reference, with only the field solver disabled; it is a timing control, not a zero-current matched-beam tune test. Its much lower cost shows that the FFT2D5 path dominates the charged pilot. The difference does not isolate FFT transforms, deposition or reference-path projection individually; profiling is needed before assigning a specific bottleneck.

The turn period is 1.179718719714e-6 s. Sixteen turns require 6,291,834 fixed steps at 3 ps, or about 4.72 million at 4 ps. The estimate uses `(native mainTimer - OrbitThreader time)/100`; a fit of ten-step progress timestamps independently agrees (0.738 s/step for four threads). Startup orbit threading is about 1.8 s. Estimates assume unchanged throughput; thermal throttling, evolved-beam behavior and long-run output can change it. This short pilot is sufficient to reject laptop feasibility at the present settings, not to validate a sixteen-turn physical solution.

## Validation

All three pilots reached t=300 ps, global step 99, with all 100,000 distinct IDs, correct total charge and finite coordinates/momenta. The charged pilots agree between one and four threads to a maximum endpoint momentum-vector difference of 7.8e-13 in beta-gamma units. Comparing charged and ZeroSC endpoints gives a maximum momentum difference of 5.38e-8, confirming nonzero space-charge action. No tune or long-term stability result is claimed: only about 0.000254 turns were tracked.

The first one-thread input attempt lacked quotes around FFT2D5 and was rejected before tracking; it was corrected without a source change. The successful one-thread pilot was wrapped in `/usr/bin/time -l`, whose kernel-clock probe was denied by the sandbox after OPALX finished. Its manifest retains wrapper exit status 1 and records completion from native timer counts and the audited final snapshot. The four-thread pilots used direct execution and exited with status 0.

The four-thread SC child reported peak resident memory of about 130 MB through `getrusage`; the timing control reported 291 MB. Memory did not prevent these pilots, but no system-wide peak or long-run memory study was performed.
